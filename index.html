MEET THE ARTIST

Where beauty meets artistry

Her journey into beauty began long before she picked up a brush. After training at beauty school in Munich, Germany, she brought her craft to California — completing advanced education at The Elite Academy in Stockton and earning her California State Cosmetology License in 2007.

She expanded her reach in 2014 with a Texas State License, becoming double-certified to serve brides across the country. With ongoing education through M.A.C. and a belief that great artistry never stops growing, she has spent nearly two decades helping women feel like the most radiant version of themselves — on the most important day of their lives.
